- When the brief names an acceptance list, judge only its items, against the threats it puts in scope. Put every other finding under Outside the list, one line each, starting with its severity, Critical, High, Medium or Low. Such a finding never fails the review. Fail an item that passed in an earlier round only with a failing test. End the report with the verdict lines, `Verdict: GO` or `Verdict: NO-GO`, then one line per item, such as `A1: PASS`, `A2: FAIL <file>:<line> <function>. <what fails>` or `A3: FAIL not checked: <reason>`.
